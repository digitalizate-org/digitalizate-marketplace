## Why
Since 79% of web users scan rather than read, the words that receive visual emphasis through bold or color function as a 'second layer' of communication — users can extract key information from a page's emphasized words without reading full sentences. NNGroup's landmark 'How Users Read on the Web' research identifies highlighted keywords (hypertext links, typeface variations, color) as one of the five most effective techniques for improving usability through scannable text. However, overuse of emphasis destroys the hierarchy: if 30% of text is bold, nothing stands out and users' scanning efficiency degrades.

## How to implement
- Bold 2–4 key terms or phrases per paragraph maximum — the terms that, if read in isolation, would convey the main point
- Use bold for genuinely critical information: key data points, product names, action steps, warnings — not for decorative emphasis
- Do not use italic for emphasis in body text (it slows reading by disrupting the regular word-shape recognition process); reserve italic for citations, titles, and technical terms
- Use color emphasis (e.g., blue or brand color) only for hyperlinks, not for non-interactive emphasis, to maintain a consistent affordance vocabulary
- In help articles and documentation, bold the first mention of a technical term or key concept in each section
- Do not use underline for emphasis (it signals hyperlink in all user mental models)
- Audit existing pages: if more than 15–20% of body text is bolded, the emphasis system has broken down and requires a content audit

## Metrics
Applying this pattern can positively influence the following UX metrics:
  - **Efficiency**: Time on task, Completion time
  - **Engagement & Usage**: Scroll depth, Bounce rate
  - **Satisfaction & Perception**: Ease-of-use score, CSAT, Confidence rating
  **How to measure**: A/B test content pages with strategic keyword bolding against unformatted versions, measuring time-to-find-key-information and scroll depth. Use eye-tracking studies to verify that bolded keywords guide scanning patterns and reduce time spent re-reading paragraphs.
