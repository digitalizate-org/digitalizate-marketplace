## Why
Jakob Nielsen's landmark 1997 web readability study found that concise text improved usability by 58%, scannable layout by 47%, and objective (non-promotional) language by 27%. When all three were combined, usability improved by 124%. However, content that is too sparse creates a credibility problem: NNGroup research also identifies high-quality writing and outbound hyperlinks to authoritative sources as key trust signals. The optimal balance is: lead with the user's need, be concise, structure for scanning, but provide sufficient depth that users who want more can find it without leaving the site.

## How to implement
- Target 50% of conventional word count for web pages by eliminating preamble, repetition, and promotional language
- Replace superlatives and vague claims ('the most powerful', 'industry-leading') with specific, verifiable facts ('processes 10,000 records per second', 'used by 50,000 teams')
- Front-load answers; link to deeper content for users who want more — this supports both scanning readers and deep readers
- Include hyperlinks to authoritative external sources to signal credibility (this also improves SEO as a trust signal to search engines)
- Eliminate introductory paragraphs that describe what the article will cover instead of just covering it ('In this article, we will discuss...')
- Audit every paragraph for 'zombie sentences' — sentences that do not add unique information and can be deleted without loss
- Use the 'newspaper test': if you cut the last 50% of the content, does the page still fully serve the user's primary need?

## Metrics
Applying this pattern can positively influence the following UX metrics:
  - **Efficiency**: Time on task, Error counts
  - **Engagement & Usage**: Scroll depth, Bounce rate, Pages per session
  - **Satisfaction & Perception**: CSAT, Confidence rating
  **How to measure**: A/B test page layouts optimized for scannability (short paragraphs, headers, bullets) against credibility-rich versions (detailed explanations, data, citations), measuring scroll depth, time on page, and post-read confidence ratings. Track bounce rates for content pages by reading-pattern optimization level.
