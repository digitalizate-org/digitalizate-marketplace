## Why
Yu-kai Chou's Octalysis Framework identifies 8 core drives behind all human motivation: Epic Meaning & Calling, Development & Accomplishment, Empowerment of Creativity & Feedback, Ownership & Possession, Social Influence & Relatedness, Scarcity & Impatience, Unpredictability & Curiosity, and Loss & Avoidance. Most products rely only on a few drives, leaving massive engagement potential untapped. Auditing your product against all 8 reveals motivational blind spots and reveals which experiences feel hollow or manipulative.

## How to implement
- Audit your current product against all 8 Octalysis Core Drives using the free Octalysis Tool at [yukaichou.com](http://yukaichou.com/) to score each drive's strength
- Identify which drives are completely absent — these represent the biggest engagement opportunities
- Balance 'White Hat' drives (top of octagon: Meaning, Accomplishment, Creativity) with 'Black Hat' drives (bottom: Scarcity, Unpredictability, Loss) to motivate without creating anxiety or resentment
- Design at least one mechanic per Core Drive relevant to your product context
- Map drives to the 4 player journey phases: Discovery, Onboarding, Scaffolding, and Endgame — different drives dominate each phase
- Re-audit every 6 months and test whether engagement changes align with which drives you strengthened
- Avoid over-relying on Loss & Avoidance (Core Drive 8) alone, as it creates short-term engagement but long-term burnout ('Black Hat Rebound')

## Metrics
Applying this pattern can positively influence the following UX metrics:
  - **Engagement & Usage**: Sessions per user, Active users, Feature usage, Session frequency
  - **Adoption & Retention**: Returning visitors, Renewed subscriptions
  - **Satisfaction & Perception**: CSAT, NPS, Satisfaction rating
  **How to measure**: Map each of the 8 Octalysis core drives to specific product features and measure engagement rates by drive category via analytics. Use NPS and satisfaction surveys segmented by which drives are most activated per user segment to identify which drive-feature alignments have the highest impact.
