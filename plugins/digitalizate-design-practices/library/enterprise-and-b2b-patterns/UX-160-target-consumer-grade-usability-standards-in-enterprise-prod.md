## Why
The gap between consumer and enterprise UX has narrowed dramatically. Teams and employees now choose their own tools — products win based on quality of experience, not sales cycles alone. Enterprise products that deliver consumer-grade speed, clarity, and polish achieve dramatically higher adoption and retention. The quality bar is: is the experience good enough that users would advocate for it to their teams unprompted?

## How to implement
- Measure against consumer app standards for response time, task completion rate, and first-use success rate.
- Create onboarding that allows users to succeed without external training.
- Test task completion with real end-users (not executives) to surface actual usability pain points.
- Apply the quality metric: "Is this experience good enough that users would recommend it to colleagues?" Track NPS and advocacy as UX quality proxies alongside task completion rates.
- Never ship UX that requires an implementation consultant to configure.

## Metrics
Applying this pattern can positively influence the following UX metrics:
  - **Satisfaction & Perception**: CSAT, NPS, Ease-of-use score, App store rating
  - **Efficiency**: Time on task, Training time, Support tickets reduction
  - **Adoption & Retention**: Returning visitors, Renewed subscriptions
  **How to measure**: Benchmark enterprise product usability against consumer-grade standards using SUS scores and task success rates. Track training time and onboarding completion rates as measures of whether consumer-grade simplicity reduces the learning curve for enterprise users.
