## Why
Motivation and effort increase as users approach goal completion — the closer someone is to finishing, the harder they work to complete it. Users who see they are 8 of 10 steps complete will push harder to finish than users at step 2 of 10. For enterprise workflows with defined completion points (onboarding, data entry, approval flows), making progress visible and emphasizing proximity to completion significantly increases completion rates. "You're almost there" at 80% complete is not just friendly — it is a psychologically effective completion accelerant.

## How to implement
- Show explicit progress indicators in all multi-step flows — not just step counts but completion percentages where meaningful.
- For long processes (10+ steps), introduce "mini completions" (completing a section/stage) within the overall flow to sustain motivation.
- Show remaining steps explicitly so users can assess proximity to completion.
- Use completion-oriented language in the final stages: "One more step" rather than "Step 4 of 5." Celebrate completions with a brief, satisfying success state.

## Metrics
Applying this pattern can positively influence the following UX metrics:
  - **Effectiveness**: Completion rate, Task success rate
  - **Efficiency**: Time on task, Error counts
  - **Engagement & Usage**: Abandonment rate, Active users
  **How to measure**: A/B test multi-step flows with and without progress indicators, measuring completion rate and drop-off at each step. Use funnel analytics to identify which steps see the largest abandonment reduction when progress visibility is added.
