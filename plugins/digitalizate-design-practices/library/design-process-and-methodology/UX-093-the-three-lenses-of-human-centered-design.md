## Why
IDEO's HCD framework evaluates every solution through three overlapping lenses: Desirability (what do humans want?), Feasibility (what is technically and organizationally possible?), and Viability (what can sustain as a business?). Breakthrough solutions sit at the intersection of all three. Most product failures occur when teams optimize for one lens at the expense of others — building technically feasible solutions nobody wants, or desirable features the business cannot sustain. For enterprise software, viability often includes organizational adoption feasibility, not just commercial return.

## How to implement
- Frame every design review with three explicit questions stated aloud: "Do users genuinely want this? Can we actually build it? Does it make business sense?" Create a visible Venn diagram artifact at project kickoffs.
- Flag any solution that satisfies only one or two lenses as a risk requiring resolution before commitment.

## Metrics
Applying this pattern can positively influence the following UX metrics:
- **Effectiveness**: Task success rate, Completion rate
- **Satisfaction & Perception**: NPS, CSAT
- **Adoption & Retention**: Account creation rate, Returning visitors
- **Revenue**: Conversion rate, Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
**How to measure**: Assess whether features designed through all three lenses (desirability, feasibility, viability) show better retention and conversion rates than features optimized for only one dimension. Use NPS and CSAT surveys at 30/60/90 days post-launch to capture sustained satisfaction.
