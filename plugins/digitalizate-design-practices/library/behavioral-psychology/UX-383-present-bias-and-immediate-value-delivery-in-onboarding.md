## Why
Present bias (also called delay discounting or hyperbolic discounting) describes humans' strong preference for immediate rewards over larger future ones — a phenomenon where people choose 'smaller sooner' over 'larger later' even when the math clearly favors waiting. In product design, this means users are more motivated by immediate value delivery than by promised future benefits. Onboarding that delays the 'aha moment' by requiring extensive setup, form completion, or tutorial viewing before any value is experienced suffers from present bias dropout. Irrational Labs' research with PayPal and Google identified present bias as one of the most common design blind spots in SaaS onboarding.

## How to implement
- Design onboarding to deliver the 'aha moment' — the first genuine value experience — as quickly as possible, within the first session
- Defer all non-essential setup steps (billing, detailed profile, team invitations) until after the user has experienced at least one value moment
- Use templates, sample data, and pre-populated workspaces to allow users to experience the product immediately, before entering their own data
- Frame rewards and feature access in terms of immediate benefit, not long-term ROI: 'See your results instantly' outperforms 'Improve efficiency over time'
- For features requiring investment (data import, integration setup), provide an immediate demonstration with sample data to show value before requesting effort
- Apply 'temptation bundling': pair required but low-motivation tasks with immediately pleasant elements (engaging copy, animation, gamification)

## Metrics
Applying this pattern can positively influence the following UX metrics:
- **Engagement & Usage**: Active users, Feature usage
- **Revenue**: Conversion rate, Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- **Adoption & Retention**: Signup completion rate, Account creation rate, Returning visitors
**How to measure**: Measure time-to-first-value and onboarding completion rate in product analytics; A/B test onboarding flows that front-load immediate wins versus deferred-value flows, tracking trial-to-paid conversion rate.
