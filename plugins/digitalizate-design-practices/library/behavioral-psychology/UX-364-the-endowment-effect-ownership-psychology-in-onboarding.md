## Why
The endowment effect, demonstrated by Kahneman, Thaler, and Knetsch, shows that people value things more simply because they own or created them — the IKEA effect extends this to things they helped build. In product design, this means that users who customize their experience, create content, or invest time during onboarding develop stronger attachment and are more resistant to churn. Critically, this perceived ownership extends to features accessed during free trials: users who integrate a tool into their workflow during a trial report the product as 'theirs,' dramatically increasing conversion. Nielsen Norman Group notes this effect underpins engagement, retention, and willingness to pay for continued access.

## How to implement
- Ask users to set personal goals, preferences, or customize their dashboard during the first onboarding session — early personalization creates ownership
- Use 'Your [dashboard/workspace/profile]' language throughout the UI to reinforce personal ownership
- Display user-created content prominently (playlists, saved items, created documents) so users see evidence of their investment
- Time paywalls or upgrade prompts after users have invested effort: a paywall placed after users have customized a design (like Canva) converts far better than one shown before
- During free trials, encourage deep integration (data import, workflow setup, team invitations) — more investment means higher perceived ownership
- When sunset features or migrate users, allow them to export or carry over their custom work to new contexts, preserving the sense of ownership

## Metrics
Applying this pattern can positively influence the following UX metrics:
- **Engagement & Usage**: Feature usage, Active users
- **Revenue**: Conversion rate, Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- **Adoption & Retention**: Account creation rate, Signup completion rate, Returning visitors
**How to measure**: A/B test onboarding flows that seed personalized data or give users early ownership (filled profiles, pre-populated data) versus blank-slate flows; compare trial-to-paid conversion rate and Day-30 retention.
