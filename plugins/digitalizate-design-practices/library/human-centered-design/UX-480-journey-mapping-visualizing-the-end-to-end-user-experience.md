## Why
IDEO's Design Kit defines a Journey Map as a framework to 'identify and strategize key moments in the product, experience, or service you're designing' — tracing the customer's arc from first awareness through repeat use and advocacy. As IDEO Partner Melanie Bell-Mayeda explains, the map should capture what happens not just during direct interaction with the product, but the context before and after as well. For web applications, journey maps expose the critical emotional and functional moments — moments of trust, confusion, delight, or abandonment — that analytics alone cannot explain.

## How to implement
- Start with existing data: analytics, support tickets, NPS comments, past research — do not start from a blank canvas
- Define the scope and persona: one map per user segment; specify the journey period (e.g., first 30 days of onboarding) rather than mapping everything at once
- Structure the map across a timeline with these layers: User Actions, Touchpoints/Channels, Thoughts, Emotional State (peak/valley graph), Pain Points, and Opportunities
- Capture what happens BEFORE users reach your web product (search intent, comparison shopping, word-of-mouth) and AFTER (onboarding completion, ongoing habits, referrals)
- Validate the map in mini workshops with real users — present the draft, ask 'does this match your experience?', and use sticky notes to capture corrections and additions
- Mark 'moments of truth' — the 3–5 interaction points with disproportionate impact on user satisfaction or churn
- Treat the journey map as a living document posted visibly in the team's space; update it after every significant research or data event

## Metrics
Applying this pattern can positively influence the following UX metrics:
  - **Effectiveness**: Task success rate, Completion rate
  - **Efficiency**: Time on task, Support tickets reduction
  - **Satisfaction & Perception**: CSAT, NPS, Ease-of-use score
  - **Adoption & Retention**: Returning visitors, Renewed subscriptions
  **How to measure**: Identify high-friction moments surfaced by journey maps, then measure task completion rates and time-on-task before and after addressing those touchpoints. Track CSAT at specific journey stages using microsurveys tied to key events.
