## Why
IDEO's design thinking process treats synthesis as a distinct, critical phase between research and ideation — not a quick step but a deliberate practice of finding patterns, tensions, and revelations in raw data. As IDEO U defines it, 'an insight isn't just an observation or fact; it's a new way of seeing something that unlocks fresh thinking.' The key technique is reframing observations from descriptive ('users find it hard to track medication') to evocative and opportunity-oriented ('users want medication management to feel effortless, not like another task'). This transforms data into a design prompt that energizes ideation.

## How to implement
- After research sessions, download raw notes, quotes, and observations onto individual sticky notes — one observation per note — before attempting any analysis
- Cluster sticky notes into themes using affinity diagramming: group similar observations together, then name each cluster with a phrase that captures the tension or opportunity, not just the surface behavior
- Distinguish between observations (what users did/said), insights (why they did it / what it reveals about their needs), and opportunities (what this means for design) — keep these conceptually separate
- Write insight statements that include the human tension: 'Users want to feel in control, but the product's automated actions feel unpredictable and alarming'
- Use the Insight Statement formula: '[User segment] needs [need] because [underlying tension or surprise]'
- Prioritize insights by plotting them on an Impact (how transformative is this?) vs. Confidence (how validated is this?) matrix — focus effort on high-impact, high-confidence insights first

## Metrics
Applying this pattern can positively influence the following UX metrics:
  - **Effectiveness**: Task success rate, Completion rate
  - **Efficiency**: Time on task, Support tickets reduction
  - **Satisfaction & Perception**: CSAT, NPS, Ease-of-use score
  **How to measure**: Compare design decision quality (measured by downstream usability test success rates) between projects using formalized insight synthesis vs. informal synthesis. Track time from research completion to design direction agreement as a measure of synthesis efficiency.
