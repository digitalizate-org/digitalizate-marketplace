## Why
IDEO's 7th design thinking step — 'Share the Story' — treats storytelling as a core design discipline, not a presentation skill. David Kelley states: 'storytelling has become one of the real skills designers have been building over the last few years.' Former IDEO design leader Mark Zeh describes design thinking itself as 'the dance between storytelling and prototyping.' For web product teams, this means using narrative structure — a character (the user), a challenge (the problem), and a resolution (the design) — to communicate research insights, prototype rationales, and design decisions to stakeholders who would otherwise disengage from data-heavy presentations.

## How to implement
- Structure every research readout and design presentation as a story: open with a specific, relatable user character and their challenge (drawn from real research participants), not with statistics or abstract user personas
- Use the Pixar narrative formula: 'Once upon a time [user context]. Every day [current struggle]. One day [inciting incident / the problem we are solving]. Because of that [what we discovered]. Until finally [the proposed solution]. Ever since [the outcome]'
- Prototype as storytelling: build click-through prototypes that walk stakeholders through the user's journey step by step, rather than presenting static mockups out of context
- Build shared empathy before showing solutions: in any stakeholder presentation, show a 2–3 minute compiled user research video before revealing design proposals — this creates emotional buy-in that data cannot
- When sharing with engineering teams, translate user stories into technical requirements using 'As a [user type], I need [capability] so that [outcome]' format

## Metrics
Applying this pattern can positively influence the following UX metrics:
- **Satisfaction & Perception**: Brand perception, Satisfaction rating
- **Engagement & Usage**: Scroll depth, Clickthrough rate, Session frequency
- **Adoption & Retention**: New visitors, Signup completion rate
**How to measure**: Track scroll depth and CTA clickthrough rates on narrative-driven pages versus standard pages using A/B testing; measure brand perception shifts via periodic NPS surveys tied to storytelling-heavy touchpoints.
