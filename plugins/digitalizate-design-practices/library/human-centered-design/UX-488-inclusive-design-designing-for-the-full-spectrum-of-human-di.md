## Why
IDEO's inclusive design philosophy — which underpins its accessibility work and its approach to designing for social impact — holds that designing for constraint expands capability for all. As the Design Kit's Creative Confidence mindset states, 'anyone can approach the world like a designer' — and this must mean anyone, regardless of ability, context, or background. The seven principles of inclusive design (comparable experience, situational consideration, consistency, user control, multiple interaction modes, content priority, and adding value) reflect IDEO's broader HCD commitment. For web applications, inclusive design is not an add-on; it is a quality indicator for the entire design system.

## How to implement
- Integrate inclusive design from the beginning of the design process — not as a final accessibility checklist but as a research and design constraint that shapes initial decisions
- Recruit users with disabilities into every research cohort: screen reader users, keyboard-only navigators, users with cognitive disabilities, and color vision deficiencies provide insights that improve usability for all users
- Follow WCAG 2.1 AA as a minimum standard for contrast, text sizing, keyboard navigation, focus indicators, and ARIA labels — and build these into the design system as defaults
- Design for multiple interaction modes: ensure all core web app functionality is operable via keyboard, mouse, touch, and voice; test with actual assistive technologies (VoiceOver, NVDA, Dragon NaturallySpeaking)
- Apply progressive disclosure to reduce cognitive load: expose only what's essential initially, with clear pathways to more complexity for users who need it

## Metrics
Applying this pattern can positively influence the following UX metrics:
- **Effectiveness**: Task success rate, Error rate
- **Adoption & Retention**: New visitors from organic search, Account creation rate
- **Satisfaction & Perception**: CSAT, NPS, Brand perception
**How to measure**: Recruit participants from diverse groups (age, ability, language, device type) for usability testing and compare task success rates across segments. Monitor organic search traffic growth tied to accessibility improvements, and track NPS across demographic segments to identify and close satisfaction gaps.
